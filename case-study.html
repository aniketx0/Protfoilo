The brief

The Sales Analytics Dashboard is an interactive business intelligence solution designed to help organizations monitor sales performance, revenue growth, customer insights and key business KPIs from a single dashboard.

The main objective of the project was to transform raw sales data into meaningful visual insights. The dashboard allows users to quickly understand sales trends, identify top-performing products and regions, and make data-driven business decisions.

"The goal was to turn complex sales data into a simple, interactive dashboard that helps management understand business performance at a glance."

Data & research

Data collection

The project started with collecting and preparing sales data containing information about orders, customers, products, sales amount, quantity, regions and dates. SQL was used to store, query and prepare the data required for analysis.

  • Sales and revenue data were analyzed using SQL queries
  • Customer and product performance was examined
  • Regional sales performance was compared
  • Monthly and yearly sales trends were identified
  • Key business KPIs were prepared for dashboard visualization

Data analysis

After preparing the dataset, the data was analyzed to identify important business patterns and performance indicators. Power BI was used to create interactive charts, graphs, KPI cards and filters for exploring the sales data.

Dashboard strategy

The dashboard was designed around three main principles:

Clear KPI visualization

Important metrics such as total sales, revenue growth, total orders and customer growth are displayed prominently so users can understand overall business performance quickly.

Interactive analysis

Interactive filters and visualizations allow users to analyze sales by product, region, customer and time period without manually processing large datasets.

Data-driven decision making

The dashboard converts complex datasets into visual insights that help management identify trends, compare performance and make better business decisions.

Week 1–2 · Information architecture

I ran a card sorting exercise with 12 participants to rebuild the navigation taxonomy from scratch. The 14-item sidebar collapsed to 6 primary destinations. Key Dashboard Features

Sales Overview

The Sales Dashboard provides a clear overview of the company's sales performance. Users can quickly monitor total sales, revenue, orders, customers, and overall business growth from a single dashboard.

Sales Analytics

The dashboard presents important sales information using interactive charts and summary cards. Users can analyze daily, weekly, and monthly sales performance and easily identify business trends.

Product & Customer Management

The system helps users manage products and customers efficiently. Sales records can be monitored from the dashboard, making it easier to track orders, customer activity, and product performance.

Results

The Sales Dashboard was designed to simplify sales management and make important business information available in one place. The responsive interface allows users to monitor sales performance from desktop, tablet, and mobile devices.

  • Sales Overview: Monitor total sales and revenue
  • Sales Analytics: View sales trends using charts
  • Order Management: Track customer orders
  • Customer Management: Manage customer information
  • Product Management: Monitor product performance
  • Responsive Design: Works across different devices

"The Sales Dashboard provides a simple and efficient way to monitor business performance, manage sales information, and make data-driven decisions."

Project Learnings

This project helped me understand how dashboard interfaces can be used to present large amounts of business data in a simple and meaningful way. I focused on clean UI design, responsive layouts, data visualization, and easy navigation.

The main goal was to create a dashboard where users can quickly understand their sales performance without having to search through multiple pages.
